FAQ

Questions answered.

What operating systems does FieldVision support?+
FieldVision currently runs on Windows 10 and 11. macOS support is planned for a future update.
Do I need an internet connection to use FieldVision?+
No. FieldVision is 100% offline. Your videos and annotations never leave your computer. An internet connection is only needed to activate your Pro licence.
What video formats are supported?+
FieldVision supports MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WMV, FLV, WebM, M4V, MPEG, MPG, 3GP, TS and more.
How does the automatic player tracking work?+
Simply select a player by drawing a box around them. FieldVision's AI tracker follows that player frame by frame and overlays their trajectory on the exported clip.
